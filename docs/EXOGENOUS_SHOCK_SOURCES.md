# Primary-source channel ledger

Research access: October 3, 2026. References were discovered or read through bounded web search and official pages, abstracts or institutional summaries. This is not a full-paper replication or a data-fitting exercise.

Every catalogue event is an offline fictional scenario. References below establish mechanisms, accounting or institutional limits; they do not attest that an event will occur or validate its magnitude, duration, co-movements or net national effect. Candidate stories and dilemmas are original author extrapolations. Low-fit concepts remain in the rejected pool with specific limitations.

No quotations are used. Each ledger synopsis is brief; repeated candidate links point to shared channel evidence rather than copied passages. Rolling data pages are cited for causal discussion, not their current numerical forecasts.

## Channel references

### noaa_disasters

Historical loss accounting covers several weather hazards; it is not a frequency forecast or monetary-policy model. [NOAA NCEI: calculating disaster costs](https://www.ncei.noaa.gov/news/calculating-cost-weather-and-climate-disasters).

### bea_disasters

Disasters interrupt production and damage assets; rebuilding expenditure does not mean destruction increased welfare. [BEA: disasters and national accounts](https://www.bea.gov/help/faq/55).

### bea_state

Canceled or postponed tourism and spending enter GDP data; aggregate net effects cannot be read from damage totals. [BEA: disasters and state GDP](https://www.bea.gov/help/faq/473).

### heat

Heat constrains outdoor work and construction investment. Long-horizon model projections are not episode coefficients. [San Francisco Fed: extreme heat and productivity](https://www.frbsf.org/research-and-insights/publications/economic-letter/2024/05/impact-of-us-labor-productivity-losses-from-extreme-heat/).

### smoke

Smoke interrupts work, schooling and housing use; exposures and losses differ across communities. [San Francisco Fed: wildfire smoke disruptions](https://www.frbsf.org/research-and-insights/publications/community-development-research-briefs/disruptions-from-wildfire-smoke/).

### quake

Business interruption can continue after shaking; substitution and adaptation affect recovery. [USGS: economic resilience after earthquakes](https://www.usgs.gov/publications/economic-resilience-lessons-shakeout-earthquake-scenario).

### oil

Production quotas, spare capacity and disrupted transport affect oil supply and price; short-run substitution is limited. [EIA: oil prices and outlook](https://www.eia.gov/energyexplained/oil-and-petroleum-products/prices-and-outlook.php).

### oil_macro

A foreign oil-supply shock affects headline prices and real income; this DSGE exercise is not the game model. [Federal Reserve: oil shocks and inflation](https://www.federalreserve.gov/econres/notes/feds-notes/oil-price-shocks-and-inflation-in-a-dsge-model-of-the-global-economy-20240802.html).

### gas

Production, storage, imports/exports, weather and substitutes influence gas prices. [EIA: factors affecting natural-gas prices](https://www.eia.gov/energyexplained/natural-gas/factors-affecting-natural-gas-prices.php).

### power

Generation costs, grid costs, weather and fuel availability affect electricity prices. [EIA: electricity prices](https://www.eia.gov/energyexplained/electricity/prices-and-factors-affecting-prices.php).

### energy_macro

Energy costs affect households and businesses; inflation expectations condition the monetary-policy tradeoff. [Federal Reserve: energy and the economy](https://www.federalreserve.gov/newsevents/speech/bernanke20060615a.htm).

### food

Food categories have different production and price paths; flock losses constrain egg production. Rolling forecasts are not copied into the catalogue. [USDA ERS: food-price outlook](https://www.ers.usda.gov/data-products/food-price-outlook/summary-findings).

### cattle

Reduced forage can induce herd liquidation; biological rebuilding makes the later supply path differ from the initial one. [USDA ERS: drought and herd size](https://www.ers.usda.gov/data-products/charts-of-note/108718).

### fertilizer

Energy-intensive fertilizer supply, trade restrictions and high input costs can constrain farm production. [World Bank: fertilizers and the food crisis](https://thedocs.worldbank.org/en/doc/b5de315c82b1a3bb32bf30057aad9b74-0320012022/original/Food-Security-Update-LXVIII-Aug-11-2022.pdf).

### food_trade

Export restrictions reduce internationally available food supply; global price effects depend on exporter size and substitution. [World Bank: export restrictions and food prices](https://blogs.worldbank.org/en/psd/how-export-restrictions-are-impacting-global-food-prices).

### pollination

Many fruit, vegetable and seed crops depend on insect pollination; cereal crops need not share that dependence. [USDA ARS: pollination and crop production](https://www.ars.usda.gov/research/publications/publication/?seqNo115=200384).

### citrus

Citrus greening damages fruit and kills trees. Proposed remedies do not establish a proven economy-wide productivity gain. [USDA ARS: citrus greening](https://www.ars.usda.gov/oc/dof/citrus-greening-is-the-end-in-sight/).

### fish

Marine heat and algal blooms can disrupt fisheries; not every warm-water event has the same ecological effect. [NOAA Fisheries: marine heatwaves](https://www.fisheries.noaa.gov/feature-story/west-coast-waters-experiencing-another-large-marine-heatwave).

### food_risks

Weather, biofuel demand, inputs and trade restrictions can interact; the catalogue does not copy current projections. [World Bank: stacked food-market risks](https://blogs.worldbank.org/en/developmenttalk/when-risks-stack-up--threats-to-global-food-markets-in-2026).

### flu

Influenza illness affects attendance and productivity. National-health burden is not an estimated aggregate GDP shock. [CDC: estimated influenza burden](https://www.cdc.gov/flu-burden/php/about/faq.html).

### longcovid

Persistent symptoms can constrain work. The magnitude and attribution of labor-force effects remain uncertain. [Federal Reserve: long COVID and disability](https://www.federalreserve.gov/econres/notes/feds-notes/long-covid-cognitive-impairment-and-the-stalled-decline-in-disability-rates-20220805.html).

### caregiving

Caregiving, illness and childcare disruptions affect labor supply; a participation loss is not automatically unemployment. [Federal Reserve: COVID and women in the economy](https://www.federalreserve.gov/newsevents/speech/bowman20220817b.htm).

### health_labor

Illness, retirement and care constraints limited participation; normalization can release labor supply and services activity. [Federal Reserve: March 2023 monetary-policy report](https://www.federalreserve.gov/monetarypolicy/2023-03-mpr-part1.htm).

### drug_shortage

Manufacturing quality problems can interrupt medicine supply. Replacement capacity and regulatory work affect recovery. [FDA: drug-shortage causes](https://www.fda.gov/drugs/drug-shortages/frequently-asked-questions-about-drug-shortages).

### drug_roots

Economic incentives and logistical/regulatory barriers can delay recovery from medicine-production interruptions. [FDA: root causes of drug shortages](https://www.fda.gov/drugs/drug-shortages/report-drug-shortages-root-causes-and-potential-solutions).

### health_supply

Medical products depend on manufacturing and distribution; notification coverage differs across product classes. [FDA: regulated supply-chain information](https://www.fda.gov/emergency-preparedness-and-response/supply-chain/supply-chain-information-industry).

### telework_health

Telework and paid leave change attendance and exposure during respiratory illness; their broader macro effect is not identified here. [CDC: respiratory illness and work attendance](https://wwwnc.cdc.gov/eid/article/26/1/19-0743_article).

### gscpi

Transportation and manufacturing indicators track supply pressure; association with inflation is not a causal coefficient for each event. [New York Fed: supply-chain pressure index](https://www.newyorkfed.org/research/policy/gscpi).

### shipping

Route diversion lengthens delivery and raises shipping costs; recorded trade timing can differ from underlying demand. [IMF: Red Sea and Panama disruptions](https://www.imf.org/en/blogs/articles/2024/03/07/red-sea-attacks-disrupt-global-trade).

### trade_cost

Imported-input costs propagate through production networks and can worsen the inflation/activity tradeoff. [Federal Reserve: trade costs and inflation](https://www.federalreserve.gov/econres/ifdp/trade-costs-and-inflation-dynamics.htm).

### trade_disruption

Tariffs, restrictions and bottlenecks affect import costs; substitution and value chains matter. [Federal Reserve: trade disruptions and inflation](https://www.federalreserve.gov/econres/notes/feds-notes/how-do-trade-disruptions-affect-inflation-20250228.html).

### trade_uncertainty

Unexpected uncertainty can delay investment and hiring; resolution is an authored reverse-channel scenario, not a measured symmetric response. [Federal Reserve: trade-policy uncertainty](https://www.federalreserve.gov/econres/ifdp/the-economic-effects-of-trade-policy-uncertainty.htm).

### chip

Critical chip shortages constrained vehicle production; firms changed product mix and sourcing to limit losses. [Federal Reserve: semiconductor shortages and vehicle production](https://www.federalreserve.gov/econres/notes/feds-notes/when-the-chips-went-down-motor-vehicle-production-and-the-2021-2022-semiconductor-shortages-20260928.html).

### bottlenecks

Input shortages and demand composition can limit production even when aggregate supply rises. [Federal Reserve: bottlenecks and prices](https://www.federalreserve.gov/econres/notes/feds-notes/bottlenecks-shortages-and-soaring-prices-in-the-us-economy-20220624.html).

### ai

Productivity improvements can expand supply and expected income can raise demand; displacement and timing are uncertain. [Federal Reserve: AI and the economy](https://www.federalreserve.gov/newsevents/speech/cook20260928a.htm).

### ai_demand

Investment demand can arrive before AI productivity gains; monetary policy does not create those innovations. [Federal Reserve: supply-side disinflation dynamics](https://www.federalreserve.gov/newsevents/speech/jefferson20260206a.htm).

### cyber

Concentrated digital dependencies and interconnected systems can spread an operational incident. [Federal Reserve: cyber risk and financial stability](https://www.federalreserve.gov/publications/2021-november-financial-stability-report-near-term-risks.htm).

### cyber_payments

Payment-service impairment can transmit operational stress to financial activity; restoration is not capital repair. [Federal Reserve: operational resilience](https://www.federalreserve.gov/econres/notes/feds-notes/an-approach-to-quantifying-operational-resilience-concepts-20220701.html).

### uncertainty

Uncertainty can delay household spending and firm investment; financial volatility can affect wealth and funding. [Federal Reserve: costs of uncertainty](https://www.federalreserve.gov/econres/notes/feds-notes/costs-of-rising-uncertainty-20250424.html).

### savings

Accumulated liquid savings can support consumption; estimates depend on the counterfactual saving trend. [San Francisco Fed: pandemic excess savings](https://www.frbsf.org/research-and-insights/publications/economic-letter/2023/05/rise-and-fall-of-pandemic-excess-savings/).

### savings_limit

Depletion alone need not cause an abrupt spending fall when incomes and credit sustain consumption. [San Francisco Fed: savings depletion and consumption](https://www.frbsf.org/research-and-insights/blog/sf-fed-blog/2024/05/03/pandemic-savings-are-gone-whats-next-for-us-consumers/).

### wealth

Spending responses to wealth depend on household characteristics; an aggregate demand pulse loses that heterogeneity. [Federal Reserve: wealth heterogeneity and spending](https://www.federalreserve.gov/econres/notes/feds-notes/wealth-heterogeneity-and-consumer-spending-20250805.html).

### rebates

Some rebates are spent and others saved or used for debt; a payment amount is not an equal demand increase. [Federal Reserve: household response to tax rebates](https://www.federalreserve.gov/econres/feds/household-response-to-the-2008-tax-rebates-survey-evidence-and-aggregate-implications.htm).

### treasury

Congressional relief was administered through Treasury/IRS. This establishes institutional ownership, not a game multiplier. [Treasury: economic impact payments](https://home.treasury.gov/policy-issues/coronavirus/assistance-for-american-families-and-workers/economic-impact-payments).

### housing_wealth

Housing wealth and spending can share common causes; credit constraints condition their relationship. [Federal Reserve: housing wealth and consumption](https://www.federalreserve.gov/econres/ifdp/housing-wealth-and-consumption.htm).

### labor

Illness, migration, retirement and sectoral mismatch affect labor availability; demand and supply can move together. [Federal Reserve: labor-market rebalancing](https://www.federalreserve.gov/newsevents/speech/kugler20250307a.htm).

### childcare

Restored in-person schooling eased childcare constraints; retirement and care remain distinct participation channels. [Federal Reserve: June 2023 monetary-policy report](https://www.federalreserve.gov/monetarypolicy/2023-06-mpr-part1.htm).

### migration

Immigration and participation affect working-age population and labor supply. Migration also brings demand. [Federal Reserve: June 2025 monetary-policy report](https://www.federalreserve.gov/monetarypolicy/2025-06-mpr-part1.htm).

### retirement

Aging and retirement can reduce participation; demographic trends are not surprise shocks without a concrete trigger. [Federal Reserve: July 2024 monetary-policy report](https://www.federalreserve.gov/monetarypolicy/2024-07-mpr-part1.htm).

### apprentice

An evaluation compares apprentices with similar workers; it does not estimate an aggregate monetary-policy shock. [Department of Labor: apprenticeship earnings evaluation](https://www.dol.gov/agencies/eta/research/publications/did-apprentices-achieve-faster-earnings-growth-comparable?lang=en).

### strikes

BLS measures major stoppages and idle time. Quarterly output and inflation signs require additional author assumptions. [BLS: work stoppages](https://www.bls.gov/wsp/).

### discount

Primary credit requires generally sound eligible institutions and pledged collateral; it supplies temporary liquidity, not equity. [Federal Reserve: discount window](https://www.federalreserve.gov/monetarypolicy/discountrate.htm).

### collateral

Collateral margins and valuations determine borrowing capacity; catalogue percentages are fictional, not current official margin tables. [Federal Reserve discount window: collateral valuation](https://www.frbdiscountwindow.org/pages/collateral/collateral_valuation).

### funding

Runnable funding and redemption promises can induce asset sales; nonbank liquidity transformation does not confer window eligibility. [Federal Reserve: May 2026 funding risks](https://www.federalreserve.gov/publications/2026-may-financial-stability-report-funding-risks.htm).

### funding_history

Past runs involved repo, commercial paper and money funds. These are different contractual funding arrangements. [Federal Reserve: November 2018 funding risks](https://www.federalreserve.gov/publications/2018-november-financial-stability-report-funding.htm).

### fdic_runs

Concentrated uninsured funding and asset exposures contributed to failures; FDIC resolution and insurance are not Fed gameplay tools. [FDIC: lessons from 2023 regional-bank failures](https://www.fdic.gov/news/speeches/2024/lessons-learned-us-regional-bank-failures-2023).

### fdic_risks

Risk review separates agriculture, property, consumer, nonbank and crypto exposures; aggregate game wedges cannot reproduce sector balance sheets. [FDIC: 2023 risk review](https://www.fdic.gov/analysis/2023-risk-review).

### cre

Property-credit concentrations, underwriting and loss allowances matter for bank resilience; liquidity cannot replace loss-absorbing capital. [FDIC: commercial-property concentration advisory](https://www.fdic.gov/news/financial-institution-letters/2023/fil23064.html).

### credit

Property and consumer-credit performance can weaken separately from stable funding; reserves address expected losses. [Federal Reserve: supervision credit conditions](https://www.federalreserve.gov/publications/2024-november-supervision-and-regulation-report-executive-summary.htm).

### stability

Valuation, borrowing, leverage and funding vulnerabilities can amplify external shocks; fire sales can turn liquidity stress into losses. [Federal Reserve: financial-stability framework](https://www.federalreserve.gov/publications/2026-may-financial-stability-report-purpose-and-framework.htm).

### bank_lending

Credit supply affects activity independently of the policy rate. Reverse-channel private repair remains an author inference. [Federal Reserve: lending standards and macroeconomy](https://www.federalreserve.gov/econres/feds/changes-in-bank-lending-standards-and-the-macroeconomy.htm).

### battery

Funded manufacturing research targets cost and material reductions. Awards establish an investigated channel, not successful commercial deployment. [Department of Energy: battery manufacturing research](https://www.energy.gov/cmei/ammto/funding-selections-platform-technologies-transformative-battery-manufacturing).

### customs

Digital procedures and single-window arrangements can expedite border movement and clearance; the game rollout is hypothetical. [WTO: digital trade facilitation](https://www.wto.org/english/tratop_e/dtt_e/dtt-tradfa_e.htm).

### shutdown

Shutdown effects are largely embedded in source data; federal services and compensation need separate accounting. No exact aggregate multiplier is supplied. [BEA: shutdowns in GDP methodology](https://www.bea.gov/help/faq/1483).

### medical_prices

Health insurance premiums are decomposed for CPI measurement. A premium increase is not a one-for-one measured medical-inflation shock. [BLS: medical-care price measurement](https://www.bls.gov/cpi/factsheets/medical-care.htm).

### credentials

Recognition and bridging arrangements can release underused migrant qualifications; implementation and health-occupation licensing differ by country. [OECD: migrant qualification recognition](https://www.oecd.org/en/publications/international-migration-outlook-2025_ae26c893-en/full-report/recent-developments-in-migrant-integration-policy_3b9ac272.html).

### covenants

An early study reports null effects with several possible explanations. Hypothetical mobility gains in the candidate are conditional, not established by this result. [Minneapolis Fed: early noncompete-ban outcomes](https://www.minneapolisfed.org/research/community-development-working-papers/the-early-labor-market-impacts-of-minnesotas-2023-non-compete-enforcement-ban).

### grain_pests

Infestation can cause economic losses in stored and processed foods. A specific national stockpile episode and macro effect remain fictional. [USDA ARS: stored-product insect research](https://www.ars.usda.gov/research/project/?accnNo=442980).

### ash_aviation

Ash can damage aircraft and force flight cancellation or rerouting, disrupting transport and economic activity. [USGS: volcanic ash and aviation](https://volcanoes.usgs.gov/volcanic_ash/aviation.html).

### drilling

Drilling and completion changes improved initial well production; initial productivity is not total recoverable output or the game coefficient. [EIA: crude-oil production efficiency](https://www.eia.gov/todayinenergy/detail.php?id=42595).

### winter_work

An employment release discusses mild winter construction gains; national inflation and potential-growth responses are additional author assumptions. [BLS: mild winter and construction employment](https://www.bls.gov/ces/publications/highlights/2006/current-employment-statistics-highlights-02-2006.pdf).

### resistant_care

Resistance creates substantial healthcare burden and treatment costs. No aggregate quarterly shock is inferred from those cost totals. [CDC: resistant infections and healthcare costs](https://www.cdc.gov/antimicrobial-resistance/causes/healthcare.html).

### gps

Transportation relies on GPS positioning and timing; interference can disrupt those services, and alternatives determine resilience. [GAO: GPS interference and resilience](https://www.gao.gov/products/gao-23-105335).

## Evidence boundaries

Adverse/favorable supply signs, purchasing-power effects and credit/liquidity distinctions have primary institutional support. Specific legal rulings, inventions, private contracts, sector concentration, national net effects and successful recoveries are fictional conditions. Specific invented breakthroughs require successful deployment as a fictional condition. Institutional funding references preserve borrower-access limits.

National effects for several rejected candidates remain broad extrapolations even where direct institutional evidence supports the underlying event: speculative computation, technology-quality setbacks, private premium repricing, navigation failure, local-tax cash flow, employer relocation, medical breakthroughs and some legal/insurance losses. Their exclusion reasons identify where more direct economic evidence or a richer model would be needed.

The selected private-capital and private-deposit normalization events are favorable relative to continuing distress. Their nonnegative risk/funding profiles cannot repair an already accumulated solvency state or create a standalone negative risk shock. Parent integration must preserve that narrative condition.
