# Gameplay-depth study

The ChatGPT Pro working document, *Fed Chairman Gameplay Depth Experiment*
(17 October 2026), recommends measuring decision value before changing the
simulation. Its source-file SHA-256 is
`589c4be4d8133a1b1188aac80e2037e674deb75d6b38889e7baefd356465c37c`.
It does not recommend shipping delayed observations, persistent borrowers or
standing policy contracts without further evidence. Runtime gameplay AI remains
cancelled.

## Existing result

The private known-seed diagnostic tested eight four-meeting policy plans,
24-quarter consequences and three calculations: conditional, realized and
mechanical continuation. The opening development run covered seeds 0–7 and
checkpoints 0–31: 256 contexts, 6,144 forks and 417,792 attempted and confirmed
native transitions, including replay and repeated baseline reconstruction.

Paired material tradeoffs occurred only at the opening checkpoint, in all eight
seeds. There were no qualifying later opening-period departures or material
preference reversals in this finite menu. This is not a proof of optimal policy,
a held-out result or evidence of enjoyable play. It does not justify making staff
advice deliberately wrong or hiding information to manufacture difficulty.

A separate known-seed-0 extension now covers all 39 registered checkpoints:
the 32 opening checkpoints plus 32, 64, 96, 128, 160, 192 and 216. Its 936 forks
completed with 44,928 attempted and confirmed transitions. Only checkpoint 0
qualified, through P3/P4 inflation-employment tradeoffs: one opening episode,
zero eligible preference reversals. All seven later checkpoints were null under
the fixed instrument. Their result cannot rescue the opening result.

The private executor now reuses one authenticated baseline per seed. Fresh
baseline generation and exact replay cost 480 transitions; the 39 checkpoint
forks and their exact replays cost 44,928. These are separate charges, totaling
45,408 per seed or 726,528 for the proposed 16-seed block, before additional
snapshot qualification. The block itself has not run. Retained-data checks
verified all 936 native metric dictionaries and matched the 768 opening records
to the earlier runner in native values and compressed bytes. Correctness and
Ponytail reviews found no remaining findings in that analysis.

A current-Main snapshot renderer also passed native checks at seed 0/checkpoint
4 and seed 1/checkpoint 12, using separately authenticated producers. The first
snapshot exactly matched the prior qualified renderer's participant output;
both preserved the baseline and career fingerprints. These were headless text
checks, not computer-use, accessibility or human recognition tests.

Unused-seed confirmation, participant recognition and sustained enjoyment remain
unfinished. A bounded audit of retained experiment metadata did not establish
unused-seed custody; missing integers alone cannot do so. Any further run without
that custody must remain exploratory. The development results do not pass the
proposed progression gate or justify adopting an information delay, borrower
mechanism, standing contract or delegation feature.

## Follow-up economic prototypes

The commitment-transmission prototype C360 completed 192 registered paths across
16 authored settings, with 30,848 attempted and confirmed native transitions.
None crossed its registered 0.10 percentage-point inflation/employment screen or
produced a qualifying preference reversal. Its stability and recovery checks
passed, but the decision-value result remained null.

The subsequent Pro specification, *Fed Chairman Next Mechanism After R0/C*, has
source-file SHA-256
`bd24c7af43c3e7dff182ff2225e5bf3c7c435f8d087c6c2dd548b75811e38453`.
Its T412 prototype separates short-rate restraint from private interest payments.
Four interest-only notes reset on a twelve-quarter calendar. Two books share the
same total principal and opening payment but reverse the order of inherited
coupons. The face amount, reset calendar, consumption propensities and other
coefficients are authored assumptions, not estimates of Federal Reserve behavior.

The comparison used an active-demand context and an unanchored-inflation context,
each with both books. Three controllers selected policy: the unchanged feedback
rule, a twelve-quarter planner prioritizing inflation, and the same planner
prioritizing activity. Forecasts used current information and empty future
innovations; the evaluator retained each context's registered future shocks.
Additional tests compared later first actions, four-meeting rate/guidance splits
and an eight-reset announcement tactic against the unchanged engine.

All 120 jobs completed: 112 forty-eight-quarter paths and eight three-quarter
extensions, each with exact native replay. The 66,096 transitions comprise 10,800
actual-path advances and 55,296 conditional forecast advances. Complete-path
readers checked every retained transition. Original-engine paths and policies
were exactly invariant to the inert book labels.

The activity screen required a 0.50 percentage-point-quarter reduction in the
first eight quarters' absolute output gaps. A safe improvement also allowed at
most 0.05 percentage point deterioration in each registered 24- and 48-quarter
inflation/employment endpoint. Efficient tradeoffs retained both alternatives
when one improved activity and the other improved an endpoint materially.
Comparisons included the complete registered set from the same starting state.

| Registered question | Result |
|---|---|
| Material choice in both macro contexts | Failed: unanchored inflation qualified; active demand did not |
| Controller response changes with inherited coupons | Material pair-by-book interactions; original-engine interactions exactly zero |
| Later choices with different first actions and their own 48-quarter tails | Qualified only in the unanchored-inflation context |
| Separate rate/guidance composition choice | No qualifying opportunity or tradeoff |
| Four-meeting and repeated-reset pricing tactics | Each changed activity loss by less than 0.09, below the 0.50 screen |

T412 therefore demonstrates a limited timing effect, not the proposed broad
increase in gameplay depth or a second kind of decision. The original engine
already offered an opening inflation/activity tradeoff in the unanchored context;
that existing result cannot be credited to the new debt mechanism.

Correctness checks also passed neutral 240-quarter paths, four fixed recovery
fixtures, permanent neutral-rate/growth changes, 384 disabled-wrapper parity
transitions and 384 complete-envelope prefix replays. Those native state
roundtrips do not validate production Career loading or save migration.
Peak native memory across the study was 269,336,576 bytes. Every job finished
within 49 seconds including its independent reader. These are offline study
costs, not interactive latency measurements.

The announcement tactics reduced early payments but did not meet the material
activity screen. They do not establish an exploitable free benefit or prove that
pricing is sound. A one-step yield proxy still sets a twelve-quarter coupon;
coherent multi-period pricing remains unqualified. Fixed nominal principal also
dilutes relative to a growing economy. These limits and the failed depth tests
keep T412 outside the playable model. Further mechanisms require a new prospective
specification rather than retuning this experiment after its results. Human
recognition, sustained enjoyment, workbook parity and release checks remain
separate unfinished work.

## Optional routine follow-up candidate

The completed Pro follow-up, *Fed Chairman: The Next Step After T412*, selects
D474-A: test attendance and explanation on the unchanged original economy.
This is not another economic-depth pass for R0, C or T.

The optional control adopts the exact staged decision, then permits up to three
benchmark follow-ups. Each uses current-state rate and matching guidance with
default response slopes. Asset and loan offers reset to zero; all three committed
portfolio switches persist. The controller receives detached current state,
current shock and current phase text, never a career or future-tape accessor.

Current funding, outstanding short loans, scarce reserves, named episodes,
nonambient inputs, 0.25 pp anchor drift or a 0.25 pp promise gap return control.
The allowance also ends after four total outcomes. Small ambient shocks alone
do not force attendance. This deliberately offers little automation in the
funding-stress career, whose small positive needs remain relevant.

Stop, Escape, editing, reports and lifecycle commands cancel follow-up. Permission
is transient, defaults off and is never saved. Automatic steps do not run previews
or open periodic reviews. On return, the ordinary preview still holds a rate;
it is not a forecast of adaptive benchmark follow-up.

The completed-path explanation reconstructs changes in the output gap from
inherited-gap decay, financial restraint, current demand, accumulated supply
pressure and deferred-demand release. Its sums cover the same one-to-four-quarter
interval as the displayed change. These are equation contributions, not isolated
policy counterfactuals. Price accounting uses the prior gap and log changes;
liquidity does not create commercial-bank equity.

The first mechanical gate passed 18 native attempts: 16 confirmed transitions,
two intentional off-grid rejections and zero unknowns. Matching ordinary and
delegated paths agree in native state, history, policies and payload; a changed
future shock/episode cannot affect the earlier path. Restore replays all four
decisions. Separate pure checks cover thresholds, malformed observations,
nonfinite computed promises, stale tokens, cancellation and hand explanations.

Run the focused regressions through `scripts/test-game.sh` with
`routine_follow_up_test`, `transition_explanation_test` or `routine_ui_test`.
The interaction fixture disables forecasts and uses simulated input; it is not
computer-use or accessibility evidence.

The separate 32-quarter pilot completed all 480 native transitions: 384 paired
actual outcomes and 96 restore transitions. All six ordinary/delegated pairs
matched exactly, with no missed declared triggers. Together with Gate0, this
registered matrix used 498 attempts, 496 confirmed transitions, two deliberate
rejections and zero unknowns. This is not a count of all development work: UI,
forecast, keyboard and release regressions ran separately.

| Context | Routine attended / 32 | Alternative attended / 32 |
|---|---:|---:|
| Quiet ambient inputs | 8 | 9 |
| Unanchored inflation | 11 | 11 |
| Funding and impairment | 10 | 10 |

Quiet routine attendance fell 75%, passing the registered 50% convenience
threshold. The source-bound headless pilot took 3.74 seconds including process
startup and peaked at 143,982,592 bytes RSS on the Apple Silicon host. These are
offline test costs, not measured input latency or incremental whole-app memory.
Human recognition and voluntary continuation remain unmeasured. No improvement
in fun or new economic discretion is established.

## Offline choice records

`scripts/gameplay-study.py` implements the document's missing record-ordering
boundary. It does not run the economic diagnostic, drive the game, collect a
human response or decide whether the product passes. It adds no game state,
save migration, dependency or network request.

Use a new absolute directory outside the repository. Preparation binds three
existing files by SHA-256: a development manifest, a participant-safe current
information snapshot, and a private lossless checkpoint. The checkpoint is
hashed, not copied into the participant snapshot. Never use an evaluator-rich
capture containing future shocks as the participant snapshot.

The manifest has exactly these fields:

```json
{
  "study_id": "development-trial-01",
  "scope": "known_development",
  "seed": 1,
  "q": 4,
  "source_ids": {"main.gd": "<actual 64-character SHA-256>"},
  "runtime_sha256": "<actual 64-character SHA-256>"
}
```

Bind all actual engine, actor, renderer and evaluator dependencies in
`source_ids`. The tool records supplied identities; it cannot certify that they
match the native checkpoint, that the snapshot excludes future information, or
that a seed is unused. Confirmatory/held-out manifests are deliberately rejected.

Run with `python3 -B scripts/gameplay-study.py ACTION /absolute/new-study`:

| Action | Inputs and order |
|---|---|
| `prepare` | `--manifest FILE --snapshot FILE --checkpoint FILE` |
| `lock` | `--record FILE`: integer `plan` 0–7; nonempty `current_signal`, `expected_effect`, `reversal_condition` |
| `rate --slot 1` | `--record FILE`: `rater`, Boolean `signal`, `channel`, `reversal`, and nonempty `reason` |
| `rate --slot 2` | Same fields; a distinct identified rater (Unicode-normalized name) |
| `reveal` | `--record FILE`: the retained evaluator result, read only after choice and both ratings |
| `feedback` | `--record FILE`: integer 1–7 `agency`, `comprehensibility`, `repetition`, `confusion`; nonnegative finite `arithmetic_seconds`; Boolean `continue_playing` |
| `status` | Inspect recorded stages, rater agreement and unverified evidence categories |

Every stage binds all preceding records. New files use exclusive creation and
read-only permissions; retries never replace a choice or a partial record. A
stale concurrent writer that loses exclusive stage creation releases its own
marker without changing the winning record.
Stage files and directory entries are synced before success is reported; a sync
failure is reported without replacing the retained record. An `.incomplete` marker
is synced, including its directory entry, before the stage is created. Failed or
interrupted writes remain quarantined; later actions refuse that trial. The
marker is removed only after all required syncs succeed. After power loss, its
removal may be lost and a successful trial may be quarantined. Retain that
evidence and start a new trial rather than infer that an ambiguous write passed. On macOS, the open
record also receives `F_FULLFSYNC` after those metadata syncs, following
[Apple's durability guidance](https://developer.apple.com/library/archive/documentation/System/Conceptual/ManPages_iPhoneOS/man2/fsync.2.html).
Other supported POSIX hosts retain file/directory `fsync`. This is a stronger
flush request, not a qualification under actual power loss.
Serialized records must fit the same size limit as inputs; oversized envelopes
fail before publication. Changed bound manifests, snapshots, checkpoints, earlier chained records or a
revealed result stop the next operation. After an interrupted write, retain that
directory for diagnosis and start a separately identified trial; do not repair
history by overwriting it.

These controls govern this CLI, not a research participant's access to the
filesystem. Timestamps, hashes and different rater names do not prove independent
custody or blinding. A deliberate rewrite of the whole chain is not prevented.
Ratings can disagree without blocking a recorded reveal; the disagreement stays
visible and cannot become a passed recognition gate. `status` never infers
progression, economic correctness, held-out qualification or blinding.

Validate the actual CLI ordering, failure and recovery paths with:

```sh
python3 -B scripts/tests/test-gameplay-study.py
```

The test uses synthetic records only. It is not a substitute for the native
fork/replay checks, the fixed participant trials or sustained playtesting.

### Follow-up input pacing

Automatic follow-up waits at least two seconds between quarters. Each processed
frame earns at most 0.1 seconds of that wait, so a stalled frame cannot consume
the input opportunity or trigger a catch-up burst. Stop, Escape, edits and
current-information return conditions remain immediate. Current metrics and the
latest outcome refresh after each automatic adoption; the conditional preview
pauses until control returns. Ordinary attended decisions have no added delay.

This changes UI pacing, not the benchmark, model or permitted policy sequence.
The actual-Main regression retains its thirteen confirmed adoptions and one
known rejection, plus a separate one-transition Window/Escape timing fixture.
Simulated frame-time checks do not prove human usability; rendered interaction
and physical playtesting remain separate evidence.

### Full-career integration coverage

`scripts/test-game.sh routine_career_test` separately checks all eight shipped
starting scenarios at development seeds 1 and 17, through 240 quarters each.
The sixteen ordinary/follow-up pairs matched in every realized state, complete
policy/history bytes and production Career restore. All 11,520 native transitions
completed: 7,680 paired advances and 3,840 replay advances, with no rejections,
unknowns or hidden observer transitions. Current episodes and funding returns
prevented automatic adoption. The seeded shocks and episode metadata were not
replaced. An altered automatic guidance decision failed the policy-parity check.
Every scenario must exercise automatic adoption after Q120, except funding stress:
its two tapes are checked for positive need in every quarter and must remain
fully attended. Suppressing late automatic steps in a non-baseline scenario fails
the coverage assertion even when the attended fallback preserves numerical parity.

This opt-in test leaves the frozen 480-transition pilot and default UI suite
unchanged. It checks numerical integration, not Main's input pacing, assistive
technology, unused-seed custody or human enjoyment. No economic mechanism or
claim of greater decision value follows from this result.
